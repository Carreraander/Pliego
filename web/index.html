Included

The pro toolkit, and all of it is free

The tools that usually sit behind a paid plan are part of Pliego from the start. There is no trial, no feature tier and no watermark on what you save.

  • Sign with your digital certificate

    Create PAdES signatures with a certificate from the Windows store or a .p12 / .pfx file, add a trusted timestamp, and validate the signatures a document already has. Need several people to sign? Send it round in order, with an audit log.

    Signed · valid Maya Collins PAdES B-LT · timestamped SHA-256 · RSA 2048

  • Recognize scanned text

    OCR runs on your PC with Tesseract. Scans become PDFs you can search and copy from, and deskew and clean-up help with crooked pages.

  • Edit text and images

    Fix a typo, swap an image or add text right on the page. Watermarks, headers and footers, backgrounds and Bates numbers are here too.

  • Smart forms that run their scripts

    Design fillable forms or detect the fields in a flat document. Pliego runs the form’s own JavaScript, so totals add up, dates format themselves and wrong values are rejected. Scripts stay inside the form: they cannot submit data, open links or touch your files.

    Hours12 Rate45.00 Total€540.00 event.value = this.getField("hours").value * this.getField("rate").value;

  • Protect and redact

    Lock files with AES-256 passwords and permissions. Redaction removes names and account numbers from the file itself, and sanitizing strips metadata, hidden layers and scripts.

  • Compare versions

    See exactly what changed between two versions of a contract, with a report that lists every text change.

  • Convert

    Create PDFs from images and documents. Export pages to images or text, or to Word, Excel and PowerPoint (beta). Save as PDF/A for long-term archiving.

  • Organize pages

    Merge, split, insert, rotate and reorder pages by dragging thumbnails, then number them with page numbers that print.

  • Batch work and scripts

    Run the same steps on a whole folder, number exhibits with continuous Bates, and index thousands of PDFs for search. The pliego command does it all from scripts.

  • Print, accessibility, measuring

    Output preview with separations and total ink, preflight before the print shop, accessibility and reading-order checks, and measuring on scaled drawings.

Privacy and security

Your documents never leave your PC

Contracts, payslips, medical records. Whatever you open in Pliego is processed on your computer, text recognition included.

  • No account, no cloud

    There is nothing to sign up for. Nothing is uploaded to a server to convert, compress or sign it.

  • No telemetry

    Pliego does not collect usage data. Its only connection is the update check, which sends just the installed version.

  • Signed updates

    Every update is signed with Ed25519 and verified before it installs. If a new version fails to start, Pliego goes back to the previous one. Turn updates off and Pliego never connects again.

Questions

Is Pliego really free?

Yes. You can download Pliego and use it at home or at work without paying: no subscription, no watermarks and no limit on pages or documents.

Is it an alternative to Adobe Acrobat?

Pliego covers the work people usually buy a paid PDF editor for: editing, commenting, filling forms, signing with a digital certificate, organizing pages, protecting and redacting, OCR, comparing, compressing and converting. It is an independent project with no connection to Adobe.

If you rely on a specific feature, look for it in the feature list or ask on GitHub.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All the work on your PDFs, OCR included, happens on your PC. Pliego only connects to check for updates, and you can turn that off in Settings.

Can I sign a PDF with my digital certificate?

Yes. Pliego creates PAdES electronic signatures with a certificate installed in Windows or stored in a .p12 or .pfx file, and it validates the signatures a document already has.

Does it run the JavaScript in PDF forms?

Yes. Pliego has its own form JavaScript engine for the calculations, formatting and validation that forms rely on. Scripts only work inside the form: they cannot submit data, open web pages or write files.

What do I need to install it?

Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit. The installer does not ask for administrator rights: it installs for your user.

Does Pliego send my documents or usage data anywhere?

No. Pliego has no user account and no telemetry. Your documents never leave your PC.

Can I see the source code?

Yes. All of Pliego’s source code is public on GitHub.
